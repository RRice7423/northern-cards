# APWA Northern Branch Digital Business Cards

A digital business card for each Northern Branch officer (APWA Nevada Chapter, 2026-2027). Cards work on iPhone and Android.

## What is in the package

- **Card web page:** the cards as one web page, with a tab for each officer, hosted on GitHub Pages. Officers open it on their phone and add it to the home screen. The source file is `index.html`.
- **Card images (PNG):** one per officer, named `APWA_Card_First_Last.png`. These are a backup for anyone who cannot open the link.

**Card web address:** https://rrice7423.github.io/northern-cards/

## What each card has

- Your name, title, company, and address.
- Call and Email buttons, each with a Copy button. If a button does nothing on your device, use Copy.
- **Save contact** QR code: the other person scans it with their phone camera and taps Add Contact.
- **Join the listserv** QR code: scanning it opens an email to Randy asking to join the Northern Nevada Branch listserv. The message starts with "Name: [INSERT NAME]". The sender replaces that with their name and sends it.

## Put your card on your phone's home screen

1. Open your personal address in your phone's browser. It is the card address plus your name, for example `https://rrice7423.github.io/northern-cards/#kelly-dustin`.
2. **iPhone (Safari):** tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, then Add.
3. **Android (Chrome):** tap the three-dot menu, then Add to Home screen, then Add.
4. Tap the new icon any time to pull up your card. Turn the screen brightness up when someone scans it.

If the icon opens the first officer's card instead of yours, tap your name at the top.

Names to add: `#randall-rice`, `#kelly-dustin`, `#rachel-cave`, `#mike-wilhelm`, `#frankie-vigil`, `#michon-reede`, `#tanner-richardson`, `#launie-mcroberts`.

## Using the QR codes

- Hold the phone camera 6 to 12 inches from the code, in good light.
- To share your contact, let the other person scan the **Save contact** code.
- On a slide, make a QR code at least 2 inches wide so it scans from across a room.
- If a scan does not work, move closer or turn up the screen brightness.

## Good to know

- Contact details come from nevada.apwa.org. Frankie Vigil's card shows only an email because no phone or address is listed.
- The Join the listserv code emails Randy for every card.
- Phone-based business card apps such as Blinq and HiHello are an alternative if officers want Apple Wallet or Google Wallet cards. They do not include the listserv QR code.

## Updating the cards

To change a name, title, phone number, or email, or to add or remove an officer, send the change to Randy. He will update the web page and send new images. The address stays the same, so officers do not need to redo their home screen icons.
