PUT IT ON YOUR HOME SCREEN
iPhone (Safari): tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, then Add.
Android (Chrome): tap the three-dot menu, then Add to Home screen, then Add.
Tap the new icon any time to pull up your card. If it opens someone else's card, tap your name at the top.

WHAT IS ON YOUR CARD
- Your name, title, company, and address
- Call and Email buttons, each with a Copy button
- A "Save contact" QR code. The other person scans it with their camera and taps Add Contact.
- A "Join the listserv" QR code. Scanning it opens an email to Randy asking to join the Northern Nevada Branch listserv. The sender replaces "[INSERT NAME]" with their name and sends it.

TIPS FOR SCANNING
- Hold the camera 6 to 12 inches from the code, in good light.
- Turn your screen brightness up.
- On a slide, make the QR code at least 2 inches wide.

IF THE LINK WON'T OPEN
Image versions of the cards are attached in the zip file. Save yours to your phone's photos.
